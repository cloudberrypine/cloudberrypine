About the Studio

Anton grew up in Sweden with a fascination for the way trees branch, compete for light, and change through time. That interest met a background in mathematics, games, music, visual art, and graphics programming.

After studying at the Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm and working as a software engineer at Smule in San Francisco, Anton spent a year travelling around the United States in a camper van. Between national parks, he built the serious prototypes that would eventually lead to PolyPine.

Anton develops the games as a solo developer, handling programming, design, art, and music.
