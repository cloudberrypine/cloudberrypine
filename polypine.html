About PolyPine

Create habitats for plants, insects, and animals, then grow a thriving forest ecosystem. Every plant develops through a simulation where each branch uses water and sunlight.

Grow every branch

Plant seeds and watch water and sunlight shape each branch as unique trees take root and create new habitats.

Trigger animal behaviors

Click animals to influence the forest. Foxes hunt, voles dig for roots, and animals spread nutrients and seeds when they poop.

Welcome wildlife

Create suitable habitats, then introduce new plants and animals when the conditions are right.

Build a resilient forest

Earn balance points through ecological interactions, add more life, and help the forest endure seasonal changes and natural disasters.

Forest Gallery

History

I've always been fascinated by trees. I first decided to create a game where you play as a tree, but when I showed it to friends and family, they were much more excited about the animals.

That changed the project from a tree simulator into a game about forest ecosystems. The tree simulation is still there, adding another layer of depth.
